## Rules for Using This File

1. **Always customize** — generic responses hurt more than help
2. **Use their exact words** — "You mentioned the pasta was perfect" sounds better than "Glad you liked the food"
3. **Respond within 24-48 hours** — fast responses show you care
4. **Keep it under 100 words** — people read review responses
5. **Never use it verbatim** — always adjust to your voice

---

## Pro Tips

**For positive reviews:**
- Reference something specific they said
- Mention staff by name if they did
- Invite them back without being pushy

**For negative reviews:**
- Acknowledge the issue first, not your defense
- Apologize without admitting legal liability
- Offer a next step (contact you, come back, etc.)
- Move complex situations offline

**For neutral reviews:**
- Thank them for feedback
- Address both positives and concerns
- Show you're listening and improving
