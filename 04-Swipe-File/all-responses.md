## Pro Tips

1. **Respond within 24-48 hours** — shows you're paying attention
2. **Use the customer's exact words** — "You mentioned the pasta was perfectly cooked" sounds more genuine than "Glad you liked the food"
3. **Keep it short** — under 100 words. People read reviews AND responses
4. **Don't delete negative reviews** — responses show future customers how you handle criticism
5. **Move the conversation offline when needed** — "Please call me at [number]" for complex issues
6. **Never use templates verbatim** — always adjust to match your voice and the specific review
