## Response Template Anatomy

Every response follows this structure:

1. **Opening** — Thank them (be specific about what they said)
2. **Body** — Address what they mentioned, show you listened
3. **Close** — Invite them back, offer next step, or give contact info

```
[Thank them for specific thing they said]
[Show you care about their experience]
[If negative: acknowledge issue + apologize + offer solution]
[Invite them back / offer contact]
```

---

## Quick Tone Guide

| Tone | When to Use | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Grateful** | 5-star, felt genuinely appreciated | "This made our whole week..." |
| **Playful** | 5-star, lighthearted review | "Okay, we're blushing..." |
| **Professional** | Business context, formal | "We appreciate your feedback..." |
| **Empathetic** | Negative review, show understanding | "I completely understand your frustration..." |
| **Action-focused** | Negative, what you're doing about it | "We've already implemented changes..." |
| **Warm** | Mixed/neutral, connecting | "Thanks for sharing — we hear you..." |

## Copy-Paste Template

```
Thank you for your feedback! [Reference specific thing they said].

[If mixed: Validate both positives and concerns]

[Invite them back / offer to contact you directly / thank them for being a customer]
```
