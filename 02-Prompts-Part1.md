# 100 ChatGPT Prompts for Local Business Owners
**Part 1 of 2 — Categories 1-3 (Prompts 1-50)**

*Ready-to-use, copy-and-paste prompts organized by category.*

---

## How to Use These Prompts

1. Go to chatgpt.com and sign in (free account works fine)
2. Copy the prompt text from here
3. Paste it into ChatGPT
4. Replace anything in [brackets] with your specific details
5. Adjust the output to match your voice

## Category 1: Review Responses (15 Prompts)

### Positive Review Responses

**Prompt 1:**
```
You own a small restaurant. A customer left this 5-star review: "[paste review]". Write a warm, genuine response that thanks them specifically, mentions something they mentioned in their review, and invites them back. Keep it under 80 words.
```

**Prompt 2:**
```
You own a salon. Write 3 different responses to this 5-star review: "[paste review]". Make each one feel personal and different — one should be playful, one heartfelt, one professional. Keep each under 75 words.
```

**Prompt 3:**
```
You own a [type of business]. A customer left this review: "[paste review]". Write a response that feels like a real human who genuinely cares, not a corporate template. Reference something specific they said. Under 100 words.
```

**Prompt 4:**
```
A first-time customer left you a 5-star review mentioning they were nervous before coming but felt welcomed. You own a [type of business]. Write a response that acknowledges their courage to try somewhere new, thanks them for letting you earn their business, and invites them to return. Warm and personal tone. Under 80 words.
```

**Prompt 5:**
```
You own a [type of business]. A customer left a 5-star review that mentioned a specific staff member by name: "[paste review]". Write a response that thanks the customer, celebrates that team member publicly, and invites the customer back. Under 80 words.
```

### Negative Review Responses

**Prompt 6:**
```
You own a [restaurant/salon/retail shop]. A customer left this 2-star review: "[paste review]". Write 3 different response options that: (1) acknowledge the issue without being defensive, (2) show genuine empathy, (3) offer a specific next step. Keep each under 100 words.
```

**Prompt 7:**
```
A customer left this negative review about your [type of business]: "[paste review]". They specifically mentioned [specific complaint]. Write a professional, empathetic response that addresses their concern, takes accountability where appropriate, and offers to make it right. Under 120 words.
```

**Prompt 8:**
```
You own a small [type of business]. A customer left this 1-star review: "[paste review]". Write a response that: is calm and professional, acknowledges their experience, doesn't make excuses, invites them to contact you directly to resolve it. Under 100 words.
```

**Prompt 9:**
```
A customer left a negative review about waiting time at your [type of business]: "[paste review]". Write an apology-focused response that validates their frustration, explains what you've done to address it, and offers a small gesture of goodwill. Under 100 words.
```

**Prompt 10:**
```
You own a [type of business]. A customer left this review: "[paste review]" about [specific issue]. Write a response that sounds genuinely apologetic, explains what changed since their visit, and invites them back to give you another chance. Under 110 words.
```

### Neutral/Mixed Review Responses

**Prompt 11:**
```
You own a [type of business]. A customer left this 3-star review: "[paste review]". Write a response that thanks them for feedback, acknowledges both their positive and negative points, and invites them back with the promise of an improved experience. Under 100 words.
```

**Prompt 12:**
```
A customer left this review: "[paste review]" with both praise and criticism. You own a [type of business]. Write a response that validates the positives, addresses the concerns honestly, and shows you care about every customer's experience. Under 110 words.
```

### Review Request Messages

**Prompt 13:**
```
Write 10 different text messages for a [type of business] to send to happy customers asking them to leave a review. Each should be under 50 words, feel personal (not automated), give specific direction on where to review, and not sound desperate. Mix of friendly, grateful, and professional tones.
```

**Prompt 14:**
```
Write an email to send to customers 1 week after their visit to a [type of business]. Include: thanks for their business, a gentle ask to leave a review if they had a good experience, and direct links to Google and [other platform] review pages. Keep it warm and brief. Under 150 words.
```

**Prompt 15:**
```
You run a [type of business] and want to ask customers for reviews in person. Write 5 natural conversation starters that feel helpful, not pushy. Each should: be under 30 words, include a specific reason why reviews help you, direct them to where to go.
```

## Category 2: Social Media (25 Prompts)

### Instagram Captions

**Prompt 16:**
```
You are a social media manager for a [type of business]. Write 5 Instagram captions for a post about [your topic]. Requirements: each caption is different in style (one funny, one professional, one emotional, one question-based, one promotional), each works with or without hashtags, each sounds like a real person not a corporation. Include 3-5 relevant hashtags at the end of each.
```

**Prompt 17:**
```
Write 10 Instagram captions for a [type of business] promotion about [your offer]. Each should create urgency without being pushy, sound natural, and include relevant hashtags. Mix the styles so they're not all the same tone.
```

**Prompt 18:**
```
You manage social media for a [type of business]. Write an Instagram caption for [your post topic] that tells a short story (3-4 sentences), ends with a question to drive comments, and includes 5 relevant hashtags. Make it feel like a real person sharing something they genuinely care about.
```

**Prompt 19:**
```
Write 5 Instagram captions for a "behind the scenes" style post about your [type of business]. Each should: show something real about how your business works, feel authentic not staged, include a relatable angle, end with a call to action or question. Add 3-4 hashtags per caption.
```

**Prompt 20:**
```
You own a [type of business]. Write an Instagram caption for a customer testimonial or success story post. Include: a hook that grabs attention, the customer's experience in their words (not yours), your response as the business owner, and 3-4 relevant hashtags. Under 200 words total.
```

### Facebook Posts

**Prompt 21:**
```
Write a Facebook post for a [type of business] announcing [your news/promotion/event]. Include: an engaging hook in the first line, the key details, a reason to care, and a clear call to action. End with a question to drive engagement. Should feel like a real update from a real business, not an ad. Under 150 words.
```

**Prompt 22:**
```
You run a [type of business]. Write 3 different Facebook post versions for the same update: [your update]. Version 1: professional and informative. Version 2: friendly and conversational. Version 3: story-based and personal. Each should be 80-120 words and end with an engagement question.
```

**Prompt 23:**
```
Write a Facebook post for a [type of business] celebrating a milestone [anniversary, 100th customer, years in business, etc.]. Tone should be genuinely grateful, not boastful. Include: thanks to customers, a brief story about how you got here, something about the future, and a special offer for loyal customers if appropriate.
```

**Prompt 24:**
```
You manage Facebook for a [type of business]. Write a week-long series of posts (7 posts) that build toward a [promotion/event]. Each post: different angle on the same offer, increasing urgency as the week progresses, ends with a different question each day to drive engagement. Include suggested posting times.
```

### Google Business Posts

**Prompt 25:**
```
Write 5 Google Business posts for a [type of business]. Each should: be under 150 words, include a clear call to action, feel timely and relevant (not generic), include a relevant keyword naturally. Topics: new product/service, seasonal offer, behind-the-scenes, customer review highlight, and a tip or educational post.
```

**Prompt 26:**
```
You own a [type of business]. Write a Google Business post announcing your [seasonal promotion/holiday hours/special event]. Include: what it is, why it matters to customers, when/where, and a clear call to action. Under 150 words. Make it feel urgent but not desperate.
```

**Prompt 27:**
```
Write a "What's New" Google Business post for a [type of business]. Announce [new offering/product change/improvement]. Include: what it is, who it's for, why it matters, and when they can experience it. End with an invitation to stop by or reach out. Under 150 words.
```

### Content Ideas

**Prompt 28:**
```
Generate 20 social media content ideas for a [type of business]. Organize by category:
- 5 educational posts (tips your customers can use)
- 5 behind-the-scenes posts (humanize your business)
- 5 customer-focused posts (testimonials, reviews, UGC)
- 5 promotional posts (offers, events, news)
For each, give a one-line description and suggested platform.
```

**Prompt 29:**
```
You manage social media for a [type of business]. Create a monthly content calendar (30 posts) for [month]. Each post: platform (Instagram/Facebook/Google), content type, topic idea, and suggested caption hook. Focus on consistency and variety. Include seasonal and local hooks where possible.
```

**Prompt 30:**
```
Write 10 "engagement bait" post ideas for a [type of business]. These are posts designed to get comments and shares, not sell anything. Examples: polls, "tag someone who...", questions about preferences, "this or that" style posts. Each should be relevant to your business and audience.
```

### Hashtag Strategy

**Prompt 31:**
```
Generate 30 relevant Instagram hashtags for a [type of business]. Organize into 3 tiers:
- Tier 1 (5 popular hashtags, 500K+ posts): [your main category]
- Tier 2 (15 medium hashtags, 10K-500K posts): [your niche + location if relevant]
- Tier 3 (10 specific hashtags, under 10K posts): [hyper-specific to your business]
Include a mix of location-based and interest-based hashtags.
```

**Prompt 32:**
```
You run a [type of business] in [city]. Generate a local-focused hashtag strategy including: 5 branded hashtags (unique to your business), 10 local hashtags, 5 industry hashtags, and 5 community hashtags. Explain when and how to use each tier.
```

### Reels/TikTok Ideas

**Prompt 33:**
```
Write 10 short-form video ideas (Reels/TikTok) for a [type of business]. Each should be under 30 seconds, show something real about your business, and have viral potential. Include: the video concept, what to show on camera, what to say, and 3-5 trending sounds or hashtags to use.
```

**Prompt 34:**
```
You own a [type of business]. Write a script for a "Day in the Life" style short video that shows your typical day at work. Include: opening hook (first 3 seconds), key moments to show, and an ending CTA. Make it feel authentic, not like a commercial. Under 90 seconds of content.
```

**Prompt 35:**
```
Write 10 "Did You Know" short video concepts for a [type of business]. Each should share a surprising or useful fact about your industry, product, or service. Include: the fact, how to present it visually, and a hook to keep viewers watching. Under 25 seconds each.
```

### Highlight Covers & Bio

**Prompt 36:**
```
You run a [type of business]. Write an Instagram bio that: tells people what you do, shows what makes you different, includes a hint of personality, and has a clear CTA. Under 150 characters. Include a list of 5 suggested highlight cover names.
```

**Prompt 37:**
```
Write an "About" section for a [type of business] that appears on Instagram, Facebook, and Google. Requirements: consistent message across all three, clear about what you do, warm and approachable tone, includes what makes you different, under 200 words each.
```

### Engagement Responses

**Prompt 38:**
```
You manage social media for a [type of business]. Write 10 different replies to use when someone comments "Amazing!" or "So cool!" on your posts. Make each one slightly different so it doesn't look automated. Keep each under 15 words.
```

**Prompt 39:**
```
Write responses for a [type of business] to use when someone asks "[common question about your business]" in the comments or in a DM. Make it friendly, helpful, and invite them to take the next step (book, visit, call, etc.). Under 50 words.
```

**Prompt 40:**
```
You own a [type of business]. A customer shared a photo of your product/service on their social media. Write 5 different ways to respond that: acknowledge them, celebrate them, and invite others to do the same. Keep each under 80 words and make each one feel genuinely personal.
```
