## ⚡ Quick Start (10 Minutes)

**Step 1 — Open ChatGPT** (free at chatgpt.com)

**Step 2 — Try your first prompt:**
```
You own a [type of business]. A customer left this 5-star review: "The staff was incredibly friendly and the service was fast." Write a warm, personal response that thanks them specifically. Under 80 words.
```

**Step 3 — Done.** You've just saved 5 minutes of writing. That's the whole point.
