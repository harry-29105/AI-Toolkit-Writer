# 🚀 Quick Start Guide

**10 minutes to your first AI-powered task**

---

## What You Need

- A free ChatGPT account (chatgpt.com)
- 10 minutes of uninterrupted time
- Your business details ready

---

## Step 1: Set Up ChatGPT (2 minutes)

1. Go to **chatgpt.com**
2. Click "Sign Up" or "Log In"
3. Use email or Google account

**Pro tip:** Before you start, give ChatGPT context about your business. Copy and paste this:

> "I own a [type of business] in [city]. My brand voice is [friendly/professional/relaxed/etc]. I serve [target customer]. Remember this for all future prompts."

ChatGPT will remember this context across conversations.

---

## Step 2: Try Your First Prompt (5 minutes)

Pick one of these based on what you need right now:

### If you have a review to respond to:
```
You own a [type of business]. A customer left this review: "[paste review here]". Write a warm, personal response that thanks them specifically. Keep it under 80 words.
```

### If you need a social media post:
```
You manage social media for a [type of business]. Write 5 Instagram captions for a post about [your topic]. Make each one different in style: funny, professional, question-based, emotional, and promotional. Include 3-4 hashtags at the end of each.
```

### If you need an email:
```
Write a monthly email newsletter for a [type of business]. Include: 5 subject lines under 50 characters, a warm opening paragraph, 3 short updates, and one clear call to action. Keep it under 500 words and write like a real human.
```

---

## Step 3: Customize the Output (3 minutes)

AI output is a starting point, not a finished product. Always:
- **Adjust the tone** to match your voice
- **Add specific details** only you would know
- **Check the facts** — AI can make things up
- **Save your best outputs** in a document for next time

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**❌ Don't use prompts without customizing them**
The same prompt won't work for every business. Adjust [bracketed text] and your tone.

**❌ Don't expect perfect output on the first try**
Treat AI output like a first draft. Edit it. Make it yours.

**❌ Don't share AI output without reviewing it**
Always read before posting. AI can make errors or say inappropriate things.

**❌ Don't try everything at once**
Pick ONE use case. Master it. Then move to the next.
