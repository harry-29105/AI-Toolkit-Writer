## Platform Strategy

### Instagram (3+ posts/week)
- At least 3 posts per week for algorithm visibility
- Use Stories daily for direct engagement
- Reels get 3x more reach than static

### Facebook (1 post/day or 5/week)
- 1 post daily or 5 per week minimum
- Include photos — posts with images get 2x more engagement
- Respond to all comments within 2 hours

### Google Business (1-2 posts/week)
- Minimum 1 post per week
- Always include photos — 3x more views
- Update immediately for hours, holidays, events

### TikTok/Reels (3-5 per week)
- Post 3-5 times per week
- Hook in first 3 seconds
- Use trending sounds when relevant

## Monthly Review Checklist

Every month, ask:
- [ ] Which posts got the most engagement?
- [ ] Which posts led to bookings or inquiries?
- [ ] Am I posting consistently?
- [ ] Is my content mix balanced?
- [ ] Am I responding to all comments and messages?
