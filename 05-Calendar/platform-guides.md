# Platform-Specific Strategy Guides

---

## Instagram Strategy

### Posting Frequency
- **Minimum:** 3 posts per week (algorithm rewards consistency)
- **Optimal:** 1 post daily + 5-7 Stories per day
- **Best times to post:** 9am, 12pm, 5pm (adjust based on your audience)

### Content Mix (Target)
| Type | % | Why |
|------|---|-----|
| Educational | 30% | Shows expertise |
| Promotional | 25% | Drives sales |
| Personal/Behind-the-scenes | 25% | Humanizes brand |
| Social Proof | 15% | Builds trust |
| Engagement | 5% | Builds community |

### Instagram-Specific Tips
- Use Reels — they get 3x more reach than static posts
- First comment should have keywords and hashtags (caption already has them)
- Respond to all comments within first hour
- Use Stories for temporary polls, questions, countdowns
- Save your best-performing post captions for reuse

### Growth Tactics
1. Engage with 5-10 accounts daily (not just comment on yours)
2. Use location tags in every post
3. Tag your location — shows up in location searches
4. Collaborate with local businesses for shoutouts
5. Reply to every DM within 2 hours

## Facebook Strategy

### Posting Frequency
- **Minimum:** 5 posts per week
- **Optimal:** 1 post daily + 2-3 engagement posts per week
- **Best times to post:** 8am, 12pm, 3pm

### Facebook Algorithm Priorities
1. **Meaningful interactions** — posts that start conversations
2. **Video** — Facebook Live gets 6x more engagement than regular video
3. **Photos with text** — posts with images get 2x more engagement

### Content Types That Work
- **Questions** — "What would you like to see more of?"
- **Behind-the-scenes** — people love seeing the real you
- **Customer photos** — user-generated content builds trust
- **Events** — if you host events, Facebook is essential
- **Live videos** — even a 5-minute "welcome to our shop" works

### Facebook Business Page Setup
1. Fill out every field (don't skip anything)
2. Upload a cover photo (use the dimensions 820x312)
3. Add a profile photo (use your logo)
4. Pin an important post to the top
5. Enable messaging (respond within 2 hours)

## Google Business Profile Strategy

### Update Frequency
- **Minimum:** 1 post per week
- **Optimal:** 2-3 posts per week
- **Immediately update** for: hours changes, holidays, special events

### Post Types and When to Use Them

| Post Type | When to Use | Example |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| **What's New** | New products, services, changes | "We've added [new service]!" |
| **Event** | In-store events, workshops | "Join us for [event] on [date]" |
| **Offer** | Promotions, discounts | "20% off this week only" |
| **Product** | Showcase items | "Here's our new [product]" |

### Photos — Do This Now
- Add 3-5 photos per week
- Include: interior, exterior, team, products, customers
- Posts with photos get **3x more views** than text-only
- Use your phone — quality doesn't matter as much as authenticity

### Reviews — The Most Important Thing
- Respond to every review (positive and negative)
- Respond within 24-48 hours
- Thank positive reviewers
- Address negative reviewers with empathy and solutions
- Ask for reviews regularly (see Use Case 8 in Playbook)

## TikTok/Reels Strategy

### Posting Frequency
- **Minimum:** 3 posts per week
- **Optimal:** 1 post per day
- **Best times:** 7am, 12pm, 7pm (TikTok audience is younger)

### The First 3 Seconds
Your hook must be in the first 3 seconds or people scroll past. Examples:
- "Here's the mistake most [business type] owners make..."
- "3 things I wish I knew before opening my [business]..."
- "POV: You're a first-time customer at my [business]..."
- "Breaking down our most popular [item]..."

### Content Ideas That Work
- **Day in the life** — show behind-the-scenes
- **Before/after** — transformations, results
- **Tips and how-tos** — share your expertise
- **Debunking myths** — "Everyone thinks X, but actually Y"
- **Trending sounds** — use what's popular in your niche
- **Response videos** — answer common questions

### TikTok vs. Reels
- **TikTok:** More algorithm-driven, easier to go viral, younger audience
- **Instagram Reels:** Better for business audiences, cross-post from TikTok works
- **Strategy:** Post to TikTok first, then repost to Reels

### Captions and Hashtags
- Add captions (Capcut does this automatically — use it)
- Hashtags on TikTok work differently — use 3-5 relevant hashtags, not trending ones
- On Reels, use 5-10 hashtags in the caption

## Cross-Platform Strategy

### Repurposing Framework
1. **TikTok/Reels** → Short quote card for IG, quote graphic for FB
2. **Blog post** → 5 Instagram posts, 2 Facebook posts, 1 email
3. **Email newsletter** → Break into 5 social posts
4. **Long video** → 3 short videos, 10 caption ideas

### Content Unpack Example
One customer story can become:
- 1 Reel/TikTok (behind-the-scenes)
- 1 Instagram post (testimonial)
- 1 Facebook post (full story)
- 1 Google Business post (review highlight)
- 1 email (feature in newsletter)

### Scheduling Workflow
**Friday: Content Planning (2 hours)**
1. Review this week's analytics (what worked)
2. Pick content for next week from the calendar
3. Write all captions (use ChatGPT)
4. Design graphics (use Canva templates)
5. Schedule in Meta Business Suite or Later

**Daily: 30 minutes**
1. Check comments and DMs (respond within 2 hours)
2. Post scheduled content
3. Engage with 5-10 accounts

## Analytics and Adjustment

### What to Track Weekly
| Metric | Why It Matters |
|--------|---------------|
| **Reach** | How many people saw your content |
| **Engagement** | Are people interacting? |
| **Click-through** | Are people taking action? |
| ** Saves** | Is content valuable enough to save? |
| **DMs** | Are people reaching out? |

### Monthly Review Questions
- [ ] Which posts performed best and why?
- [ ] Which platform drives the most engagement?
- [ ] Am I posting consistently on my chosen platforms?
- [ ] Is my content mix balanced?
- [ ] Are my captions and hooks working?
- [ ] What should I do differently next month?
