- **Content Types:** Edu = Educational, Pro = Promotional, Per = Personal/Behind-the-scenes, Soc = Social Proof, Eng = Engagement
- **Adapt freely** — this is a framework, not a rulebook

## Content Mix Guidelines

To keep your feed balanced and engaging, aim for this mix:

| Content Type | Target % | Example |
|--------------|----------|---------|
| Educational | 30% | Tips, how-tos, industry insights |
| Promotional | 25% | Products, services, offers |
| Personal/Behind-the-scenes | 25% | Team, stories, day-in-the-life |
| Social Proof | 15% | Reviews, testimonials, milestones |
| Engagement | 5% | Polls, questions, caption contests |

## Platform-Specific Best Practices

### Instagram
- Post at least 3x per week for algorithm visibility
- Use Stories daily for direct engagement
- Reels get 3x more reach than static posts
- Respond to comments within first hour

### Facebook
- Post 1x daily or 5x per week
- Facebook lives get 6x more engagement than regular video
- Posts with images get 2x more engagement
- Respond to all comments and messages within 2 hours

### Google Business
- Post 1-2x per week minimum
- Include photos in every post (posts with photos get 3x more views)
- Update immediately for hours changes, holidays, events
- Use Offers posts for promotions (trackable)

### TikTok/Reels
- Post 3-5x per week
- First 3 seconds are critical — hook immediately
- Use trending sounds when relevant
- Duet or stitch competitor content to join trending conversations

## Monthly Review Checklist

Every month, ask yourself:
- [ ] Which posts got the most engagement?
- [ ] Which posts led to bookings or inquiries?
- [ ] Are you posting consistently on your chosen platforms?
- [ ] Is your content mix balanced (not all sales, not all personal)?
- [ ] Are you responding to all comments and messages?
- [ ] What does your analytics say about optimal posting times?
