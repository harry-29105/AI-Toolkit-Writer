## Pro Tips

1. **Save your variations** — once you customize a prompt for your business, save it
2. **Give ChatGPT context** — "I own a [type] in [city]" makes outputs much better
3. **Iterate, don't start over** — "Make it more [X]" usually works better than re-prompting
4. **Review before posting** — AI outputs are drafts, always edit before publishing
