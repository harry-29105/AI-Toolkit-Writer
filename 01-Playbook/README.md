## How to Use This Playbook

1. **Pick a use case** that matches something you already spend time on
2. **Copy the prompt** exactly as shown
3. **Paste into ChatGPT** and replace [bracketed text] with your details
4. **Adjust the output** to match your voice
5. **Save your best outputs** — they'll improve over time
