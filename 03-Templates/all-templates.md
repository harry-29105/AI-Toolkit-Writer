# Canva Templates Guide
**30 Plug-and-Play Branded Posts for Instagram, Facebook & Google Business**

---

## How to Use This Guide

Each template below is designed to work with Canva's free tier. Every template has:
- **Dimensions** optimized for the platform
- **Placeholder text** you replace with your business details
- **Color palette** that can be customized to match your brand
- **Instructions** on what to change and what to keep

To use: Go to canva.com, create free account, search for the template name, customize, download, post.
