## Pro Design Tips

1. **Fonts:** Use max 2 fonts per design. One for headings, one for body.
2. **Colors:** Pick 1 main color + 1 accent + black/white. Stick to it.
3. **White space:** Don't crowd designs. Let elements breathe.
4. **Photos:** Use your own photos when possible. Canva photos work too.
5. **Consistency:** Your feed should look recognizable at a glance.
