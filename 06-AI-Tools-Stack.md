# AI Tools Stack Card
**8 Free & Cheap AI Tools Every Local Business Owner Needs**

---

## How to Use This Card

These are the tools we recommend after testing dozens. All have free tiers or trials. No tech skills required — each can be set up in under 15 minutes.

Keep this card handy. When you need to do something new, check which tool fits.

---

## Tool 1: ChatGPT (Free Tier Available)

**What it does:** Your AI assistant for writing, brainstorming, drafting, and problem-solving.

**Best for:**
- Writing social media posts and captions
- Drafting review responses
- Creating email newsletters
- Generating blog content
- Brainstorming new offerings

**How to get started:**
1. Go to chatgpt.com
2. Sign up with email or Google account
3. Start typing prompts (use the prompts from this toolkit!)

**Cost:** Free (GPT-4 available on paid plan at $20/mo — worth it if you use it daily)

**Pro tip:** Give ChatGPT context about your business once, then it remembers: "I own a [type] in [city]. Here's my brand voice: [description]. Remember this for all future prompts."

---

## Tool 2: Canva (Free Tier Available)

**What it does:** Design tool for creating social media graphics, presentations, logos, and print materials without design skills.

**Best for:**
- Instagram and Facebook posts
- Google Business photos
- Menu designs
- Business cards
- Promotion graphics

**How to get started:**
1. Go to canva.com
2. Sign up free
3. Search templates by platform and content type
4. Customize with your brand colors and text
5. Download and post

**Cost:** Free (Canva Pro is $13/mo — has more templates, brand kit, and one-click resize)

**Pro tip:** Use the "Brand Kit" feature to save your colors and fonts. Makes every post consistent in minutes.

---

## Tool 3: Google Business Profile (Free)

**What it does:** Your business listing on Google Maps and Search. Shows up when people search for businesses like yours.

**Best for:**
- Showing up in local search results
- Managing reviews in one place
- Posting updates and offers
- Sharing photos of your business
- Getting directions and calls from customers

**How to get started:**
1. Go to business.google.com
2. Claim or create your business listing
3. Fill out every field (hours, services, photos, description)
4. Verify via mail or phone

**Cost:** Free (always)

**Pro tip:** Post to your GBP at least once a week using the templates from this toolkit. Posts with photos get 3x more views.

---

## Tool 4: Capcut (Free Tier Available)

**What it does:** Video editing app that makes creating short-form content (Reels, TikTok) easy, even for beginners.

**Best for:**
- Editing Reels and TikTok videos
- Adding captions automatically (game changer)
- Cutting and trimming clips
- Adding transitions and effects
- Creating short promotional videos

**How to get started:**
1. Download Capcut (mobile or desktop)
2. Import your video clips
3. Use templates or edit manually
4. Add auto-captions with one click
5. Export and post

**Cost:** Free (Capcut Pro available but not required)

**Pro tip:** Auto-generated captions increase watch time by 40%. Always add captions to your videos.

---

## Tool 5: Notion (Free Tier Available)

**What it does:** All-in-one workspace for notes, task management, databases, and planning. Replace your scattered Google Docs and sticky notes.

**Best for:**
- Planning content calendars
- Tracking customer interactions
- Organizing SOPs and processes
- Managing projects and to-do lists
- Building knowledge bases

**How to get started:**
1. Go to notion.so
2. Sign up free
3. Start with a blank page or use a template
4. Create databases for what you need
5. Invite team members if relevant

**Cost:** Free for personal use (teams from $8/user/mo)

**Pro tip:** Use their templates for content calendars and CRM — saves hours of setup time.

---

## Tool 6: Google Calendar (Free)

**What it does:** Scheduling tool that blocks time for deep work, client appointments, and content creation blocks.

**Best for:**
- Blocking "content creation time" each week
- Scheduling client appointments
- Setting reminders for posting days
- Blocking focus time (no meetings)
- Syncing across devices

**How to get started:**
1. Open Google Calendar (calendar.google.com)
2. Create calendars for different areas (business, personal, content)
3. Set up recurring events for regular tasks
4. Use "Goals" feature to block time automatically

**Cost:** Free (requires Google account — same one you use for Business Profile)

**Pro tip:** Block 2 hours every Friday afternoon for content planning. Put it on the calendar first, protect it like an appointment.

---

## Tool 7: Textmagic or Google Voice (Free Tier Available)

**What it does:** Business texting tool that lets you text customers from a business number without using your personal phone.

**Best for:**
- Appointment reminders via text
- Quick responses to customer inquiries
- Sending review requests
- Confirming bookings
- Updates and announcements

**How to get started (Textmagic):**
1. Go to textmagic.com
2. Sign up for free trial
3. Get a business number
4. Import contacts or add manually
5. Send texts from the app or web dashboard

**How to get started (Google Voice):**
1. Go to voice.google.com
2. Sign up with your Google account
3. Get a free business number
4. Text from the app or web

**Cost:** Google Voice is free. Textmagic starts at $25/mo for business texting.

**Pro tip:** Automated appointment reminders reduce no-shows by 30%. Set up a simple text reminder 24 hours before appointments.

---

## Tool 8: Loom (Free Tier Available)

**What it does:** Screen and camera recording tool. Record yourself explaining things and share via link — no uploading needed.

**Best for:**
- Recording how-to tutorials for customers
- Sending video responses to customer questions
- Creating onboarding videos for new clients
- Recording quick updates for your team
- Making personal video emails

**How to get started:**
1. Go to loom.com
2. Sign up free
3. Install the Chrome extension or desktop app
4. Click "Record" and choose screen, camera, or both
5. Share the generated link instantly

**Cost:** Free (up to 25 videos, 5 min max each). Loom Business from $12.50/mo for unlimited.

**Pro tip:** Record a 2-minute "how to book your first appointment" video and embed it on your website or send via text. Saves you from explaining it repeatedly.
