## Minimum Viable Stack

You don't need all 8 to start. Use these 3 first:

1. **ChatGPT** — Your writing assistant (everything starts here)
2. **Canva** — Your design tool (makes you look professional)
3. **Google Business Profile** — Your local visibility (gets you found)

These 3 cover 80% of what a local business needs.

## How to Get Started

### Step 1: Set Up Your Essential Accounts (30 minutes)

- [ ] ChatGPT → chatgpt.com
- [ ] Canva → canva.com
- [ ] Google Business Profile → business.google.com

### Step 2: Set Up Your Time Blocking (15 minutes)

- [ ] Google Calendar → calendar.google.com
- [ ] Block 2 hours every Friday for content planning
- [ ] Block 30 min daily for social media management

### Step 3: Add Tools as You Need Them

- [ ] Capcut (when you need video)
- [ ] Notion (when you need better organization)
- [ ] Google Voice (when you need business texting)
- [ ] Loom (when you need to record tutorials)
